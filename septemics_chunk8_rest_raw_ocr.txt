Philosophy is a constructive, organized and analytical way of looking at things. This work seeks to make sense of human
activities, so as to improve them by finding one's level, and, when possible, moving up to the next level. In this sense, each scale is a
sort of intellectual lever. Furthermore, if you know the level, and the direction of movement, you can predict what a person or
group is likely to do in the immediate future, as you will know what level they are about to manifest. In this sense, each scale is a
sort of intellectual crystal ball.
Most of the population of Earth are at, or near, the bottom of most of these scales, because Life, as we know it, has a tendency to
drive one down these scales. When a person experiences a traumatic or catastrophic event, (s)he often tumbles down several levels,
and several scales, all at once. When a person "crashes," this is what has happened. This book would help one to climb back up.
The further down the scale, the more difficult the climb. Do not wait until it is too late. The best time to use these materials would
be as soon as possible.
Frankly, it would be difficult to exaggerate the potential of this subject to facilitate the success of its users, and bring order to a
society that never quite seems to get to its feet. Perhaps these data will fall by the wayside, and never become widely known. One
can only guess at how many times the wheel might have been invented before it finally caught on, or how many billions of lives
were wrecked because workable technologies and philosophies were lost. The ancient Romans had functioning steam engines, but
steam engines did not proliferate until James Watt, over a millennium later. Had Roman steam technology proliferated, the lingua
franca today would still be Latin.
The demographics of these scales break down as follows: The majority of people are at, or near, the bottom of most of these
scales. This work will not assist them, unless a more capable person utilizes it to assist them, which would be rare, because the
persons at, or near, the bottom of most of these scales generally would not permit it. The tiny percentage of the population that are
at, or near, the top of most of these scales are so few as to be marginal and insignificant. So, most of the benefit of this book would
be to the minority of persons at, or near, the middle of most of these scales.
Being at, or near, the bottom of any one of these scales is generally both an effect and cause of being at, or near, the bottom of
any other of these scales. Similarly, being at, or near, the top of any one of these scales is generally both an effect and cause of being
at, or near, the top of any other of these scales. In other words, there are generally upscale persons and downscale persons.
Moreover, the upscale persons tend to form groups, as do the downscale persons. Generally, the upscale groups and downscale
groups do not voluntarily associate with one another, except in conflict; or, to put it another way, birds of a feather flock together.
