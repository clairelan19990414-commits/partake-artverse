@fakeplasticbrands

@fakeplasticbrands argues that creativity is a disciplined, learnable practice fueled by deliberate research, cultural exposure, and cross-disciplinary thinking — not innate talent. The creator packages applied creative frameworks (ideation methods, pitch skills, taste cultivation) alongside curated cultural references (design archives, film, print). The through-line is that sharpening perception and building a personal visual/intellectual archive is what separates interesting work from generic output.
