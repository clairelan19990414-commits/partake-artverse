AI writing detection

A set of learnable signals — em dashes, 'puffery,' fake balance structures, hallmark words like 'delve,' curly quotes, fabricated citations — that reliably distinguish AI-generated prose from human writing.

concept 05 · DXKY8s5Dr4F

Claude Skills (portable AI identity)

Custom, reusable instruction profiles trained on brand assets (slides, fonts, colours) and saved inside Claude, so the model applies consistent style and rules across future sessions without re-prompting.

concept 06 · DVZb9Z1CF8p

AI policy lag

The structural gap between how fast AI capabilities grow and how slowly law and regulation can catch up — meaning only the companies that built the models can currently make reliable safety judgements about them.

concept 07 · DWmmBSiDmQp

AI slop and the creativity rebound

The thesis that formulaic AI-generated content is forcing human creators toward more experimental, rule-breaking work — mirroring how photography once pushed painters away from realism toward Impressionism and Dadaism.
