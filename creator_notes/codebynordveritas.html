INSTAGRAM SOURCE NOTES · BRAND SYSTEMS

@codebynordveritas

CODE by Nord Veritas argues that culture — art, architecture, institutions, collections — is never merely aesthetic but always a mechanism for signaling and consolidating capital, status, and power. The channel's method is structural decoding: stripping the surface language of taste and access to reveal the systems underneath. Its implicit claim is that power operates most effectively when it becomes invisible, naturalized as culture.

Power transmission through proximity

Durable power moves through methods and systems, not individuals — figures fade but their operational logics persist, transmitted quietly through networks rather than taught publicly.

Permission as infrastructure

The AI-in-art debate is not about aesthetics or ethics but about which systems — markets, institutions, gatekeepers — hold authority to decide what counts as legitimate cultural production.

The art world did not become financialized recently — Renaissance commissions already specified pigment grades, payment phases, and reputational signaling; only the language has changed.
