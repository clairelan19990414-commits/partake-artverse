@kai_rehagen

@kai_rehagen is a visual-first creator who treats images as autonomous art objects, deploying radical caption minimalism — blank text, deadpan generic titles, or a single emoji — as a deliberate refusal of platform content conventions and algorithmic legibility.

Vernacular titling / deadpan irony

Using the most generic possible label ('Some Pictures') as an ironic negation of branding, echoing the flat titling conventions of photobooks and exhibition catalogs.

Reducing the entire communicative gesture of a caption to a single glyph, preserving affect while stripping all descriptive or contextual language.

Platform refusal

A conscious rejection of social media content conventions — no narrative, no call-to-action, no hook — that repositions the feed as exhibition space.

Positions

A single flower emoji (💐) contains more affective precision than any descriptive caption could.
